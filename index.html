Vibration Playground v0.21

These are simulations of wave phenomena in solid materials, designed to demonstrate how sound and vibration works while being fun and musical to play with. Best viewed on a desktop or laptop computer.

Mass
Coupled oscillators on springs. Add masses, toggle coupling, drag and release. Invidual masses begin to move in wave patterns.
String
A vibrating string you can pluck and strike. Wave propagation slow enough to watch, with sound at audible pitch independently.
Lattice
A 2D triangular grid of coupled oscillators. Tonnetz pitch geometry — you can hear the shape of the lattice as waves propagate through it.
Membrane
A vibrating rectangular membrane. In development.
Beam
A vibrating beam with tools for changing shape. In development

Built with p5.js, Tone.js, and math.js.
